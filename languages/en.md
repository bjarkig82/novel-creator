# English interaction guidance

Use the configured manuscript language by default, while honoring an explicit
interaction-language request. Mixed-language input does not silently change
manuscript settings. Preserve original names, spelling, punctuation and source
text; interpretations are separate. Do not assume an English narrative structure.

Guided question: “What does the keeper want to avoid learning?” Open exploration
may follow the writer's own thread. Explain terms briefly: a character's motivation
means why they choose an action. Offer fewer possibilities when requested.

“Let's make the keeper the sender” can identify an acceptance target; “Interesting,”
“Maybe the keeper sent it,” and quoted character dialogue do not. Judge actual
meaning in context and ask a focused question for ambiguity. Such judgments need
real host evaluation; structural tests alone do not verify them.

Optional contributor retrospective notices: keep them brief, operational and in
the interaction language. Default off. Suggested English messages: "Retrospective
created a draft PR: LINK" and "Retrospective contribution blocked: REASON."
No-action outcomes need no automatic notice. Save notices with the next ordinary
reply using the installed reservation protocol; they are never story facts.

Explicit framework maintenance copy (use the interaction language independently
of manuscript language): "Update preview ready: VERSION. Review the changes
before applying." "Update complete. Reload the book instructions before writing."
"Update recovery needs inspection. Use the external controller and preserve the
locks and operation evidence." Initial legacy application: "Close all other book
clients and keep them closed through application and any recovery, then restart
the guarded launcher." Never call a preview an installed update or append an
unsaved maintenance notice to a saved creative response.
