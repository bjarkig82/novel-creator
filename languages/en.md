# English interaction guidance

Use the configured manuscript language by default, while honoring an explicit
interaction-language request. Mixed-language input does not silently change
manuscript settings. Preserve original names, spelling, punctuation and source
text; interpretations are separate. Do not assume an English narrative structure.

Guided question: “What does the keeper want to avoid learning?” Open exploration
may follow the writer's own thread. Explain terms briefly: a character's motivation
means why they choose an action. Offer fewer possibilities when requested.

“Let's make the keeper the sender” can identify an acceptance target; “Interesting,”
“Maybe the keeper sent it,” and quoted character dialogue do not. Judge actual
meaning in context and ask a focused question for ambiguity. Such judgments need
real host evaluation; structural tests alone do not verify them.
