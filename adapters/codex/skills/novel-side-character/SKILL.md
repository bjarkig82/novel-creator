name: novel-side-character
description: Use when the writer requests a complete side, supporting or minor character profile in a legacy novel, with a short intake and explicit profile acceptance.
---

Read `.novel-creator/workflows/entry.md`, then follow
[side-character profiles](../../../.novel-creator/workflows/side-character.md).
Use the selected novel's verified runtime and pinned launcher. Read fresh
source-backed context, ask only missing basics one at a time, then offer one
complete provisional profile. Retain exact current-input capture and sourced
save-before-display on every legacy turn. One clear “save this profile” accepts
and saves the current complete profile; no repeated approval. Revisions alone
do not accept generated details. No manuscript edits or automatic next character.
If the pinned workflow or legacy runtime is unavailable, explain the limit and
preserve files; do not substitute notebook persistence or reinitialize the book.
