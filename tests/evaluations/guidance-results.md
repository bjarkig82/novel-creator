# Guidance results

Structural tests verify guided/open mode, next-exchange topic changes, retained
prior state, explicit reopen requests, installed source-backed context and stable
session IDs. The shared workflow covers accessible coaching, proportional
suggestions, voice transcription input and review preference boundaries.

The installed-command journey in
[test_guidance_journey.py](../integration/test_guidance_journey.py) saves four
prepared turns through the novel-local CLI in fresh subprocesses. It checks
guided/open/guided changes and topic redirection in the same committed exchange,
retention of earlier proposals, persistent reduced-suggestion preferences, exact
Unicode/newline message readback, current-message sources and matching save/read
checkpoints. Authored replies exercise storage; they are not observed model
responses or evidence of coaching quality, display timing or pause compliance.

Actual product host runs of G01–G12 have not been collected. The earlier T103
fixture gate proves its scoped capture protocol, not creative guidance quality
under these new installed resources. T027/T113 and their convergence equivalents
remain incomplete until their actual response evidence and human requirements
are satisfied. See [cases](guidance-cases.md) and [novice requirement](novice-guidance.md).
